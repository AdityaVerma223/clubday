# ClubDay — Campus Club Management Platform

A centralized platform for managing campus clubs, student memberships, and club activities at Mirai School of Technology.

ClubDay aims to simplify student participation in campus communities by bringing club discovery, membership workflows, activity coordination, and administrative management into one platform.

## Project Overview

Managing campus clubs involves multiple activities, including maintaining club information, handling student membership requests, coordinating events, and tracking participation.

ClubDay provides a unified interface for students, coordinators, and administrators, helping organize these processes through role-oriented dashboards and dedicated workflows.

## Problem Statement

**Problem 5 — Club Activities Tracker Challenge: Student Proposal**

Campus club activities can involve fragmented communication, manual membership management, and difficulty coordinating events and student participation.

ClubDay addresses these challenges by providing a centralized platform for club discovery, membership management, and activity coordination.

## Key Features

### Student Features
- Browse available campus clubs.
- Explore club information and activities.
- Submit requests to join clubs.
- Access relevant club and activity information.

### Coordinator Features
- Manage club-related information and membership workflows.
- Review student membership requests.
- Coordinate activities and maintain relevant participation information.

### Administrator Features
- Manage club information.
- Access administrative interfaces for club management.
- Support centralized oversight of campus club activities.

### Activity Management
- View scheduled activities through the calendar.
- Access activity-related information.
- Support organized coordination of campus events.
